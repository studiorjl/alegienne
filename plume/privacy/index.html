privacy policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Alegienne ("we", "us", "our") is a small, hand-blended botanical apothecary based in Australia. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information when you visit this website, place an order, or write to us.

information we collect

When you order or enquire — by email, or through a link on this site — we collect the details you send us: your name, email address, and anything you tell us about your order (such as which blend or batch you'd like).

If you purchase through a third-party checkout (for example Shopify, Stripe or Square), that provider processes your payment directly. We do not collect or store your card or bank details ourselves.

Our website host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance — this is not used to identify you personally.

how we use it

We use your information only to prepare and send your order, to reply to your enquiry, and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.

third parties

Your information may pass through our email provider and, where relevant, our payment or checkout provider — each handling your data under their own privacy practices.

data retention & security

We keep order and enquiry details only as long as needed to fulfil your order and meet our record-keeping obligations. We take reasonable care to protect your information, though no method of transmission over the internet is ever completely secure.

your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time, by contacting us below. This policy is informed by the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, where relevant, comparable laws such as the GDPR.

changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version always lives on this page.

contact

Questions about your information: orders@alegienne.com.au
